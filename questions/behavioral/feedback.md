# Prompt

Describe useful feedback that was hard to hear. How did you evaluate and apply it? Do not disclose confidential company or personal information.

# What This Explores

Specific context, the candidate's own decisions/actions, collaboration, measurable or observable outcomes, and reflection.

# Strong Evidence Indicators

+- A concrete example with clear personal contribution and relevant constraints.
+- Honest tradeoffs, impact, learning, and what the candidate would repeat or change.
+
# Probing Questions
+
+- What did you personally do, and what alternatives did you consider?
+- What was the outcome, and how did you know?
+- What did you learn or change afterward?
+
# Hints
+
+## Hint 1
+
+Structure the response as situation, task, action, result, and reflection.
+
+# Evaluation Criteria
+
+- **Strong evidence:** Specific, reflective, appropriately accountable, and demonstrates the target competencies.
+- **Adequate evidence:** Clear example and actions with some outcome/reflection detail.
+- **Partial evidence:** Relevant story but vague ownership, evidence, or learning.
+- **Insufficient evidence:** No concrete example or answer relies on unsupported generalities.
+
+# Common Mistakes
+
+- Speaking only as “we,” blaming others, or omitting outcome and reflection.
+
+# Follow-up Questions
+
+- How would someone with a different perspective describe this situation?

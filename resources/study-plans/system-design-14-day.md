# Plan

1. Requirements, scale, and explicit assumptions.
2. API/event/data contracts.
3. State ownership and consistency.
4. Caching and invalidation.
5. Reliability, retry, idempotency, backpressure.
6. Performance and capacity.
7. Security/privacy and review.
8. Accessibility, observability, and testing.
9. Design the [dashboard](../../questions/system-design/frontend/dashboard.md).
10. Design the [feed](../../questions/system-design/frontend/social-feed.md).
11. Design the [collaborative editor](../../questions/system-design/frontend/collaborative-editor.md).
12. Design the [offline-first app](../../questions/system-design/frontend/offline-first.md).
13. Practice a backend scenario to strengthen adjacent contracts.
14. Run the [frontend system-design interview](../../interviews/system-design/frontend-system-design-60.md).

Use the [system design cheatsheet](../../cheatsheets/system-design/system-design-interview.md) as a self-review checklist, not a rigid script.

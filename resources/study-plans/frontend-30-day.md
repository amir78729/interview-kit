# Structure

- **Days 1–6:** JavaScript language/runtime; attempt two linked questions daily.
- **Day 7:** review and retrieval.
- **Days 8–13:** browser, networking, HTML, CSS, accessibility, security.
- **Day 14:** run [junior](../../interviews/frontend/frontend-junior-45.md) or [intermediate](../../interviews/frontend/frontend-intermediate-60.md) interview according to target.
- **Days 15–20:** React and TypeScript; build explanations from first principles.
- **Day 21:** review private evidence and retry gaps.
- **Days 22–25:** frontend architecture and [performance sheet](../../cheatsheets/general/frontend-performance.md).
- **Days 26–27:** algorithms from [core patterns](../../topics/algorithms/core-patterns.md).
- **Day 28:** [frontend system design interview](../../interviews/system-design/frontend-system-design-60.md).
- **Day 29:** targeted recovery and behavioral examples.
- **Day 30:** [mixed interview](../../interviews/mixed/mixed-frontend-75.md), then a private next-step plan.

# Measurement

Track whether you can explain, apply, compare, and recover—not pages read. Keep all scores and reflections in `.personal/`.

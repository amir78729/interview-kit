# How to Use

Budget 90–150 focused minutes daily. Attempt questions before reading answers and store personal evidence only in `.personal/progress.md`.

# Schedule

1. **JavaScript model:** [cheatsheet](../../cheatsheets/javascript/javascript-interview.md), then closures, `this`, and [event loop](../../questions/frontend/javascript/event-loop.md).
2. **Browser:** rendering, [HTTP cache](../../questions/frontend/browser/http-caching.md), CORS, and security.
3. **React:** rendering, state, effects, keys, and [performance](../../questions/frontend/react/performance.md).
4. **CSS/HTML/a11y:** layout sheets plus [accessible control](../../questions/frontend/browser/accessibility.md).
5. **Architecture:** state/data/cache questions and [system design sheet](../../cheatsheets/system-design/system-design-interview.md).
6. **Practice:** run [mixed frontend interview](../../interviews/mixed/mixed-frontend-75.md); record evidence privately.
7. **Targeted review:** retrieve weakest concepts, retry two questions, and prepare candidate questions.

# Completion Outcome

One realistic interview, evidence across core domains, and a private prioritized gap list—not a promise to “cover everything.”

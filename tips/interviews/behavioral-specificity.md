# Content

Use one concrete situation, distinguish your actions from the team’s, quantify or describe the outcome, and finish with reflection. Remove confidential details without becoming vague.

# When to Use

Behavioral and leadership interviews.

# Example

Apply the action in one or two sentences, then return to the interview task rather than over-explaining the technique.

# Content

Attempt a question from memory, compare against the rubric, record the gap privately, and revisit it after increasing intervals. Mix related topics to practice choosing an approach.

# When to Use

Between mock interviews and during a study plan.

# Example

Apply the action in one or two sentences, then return to the interview task rather than over-explaining the technique.
